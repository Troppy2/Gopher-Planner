# Code of Conduct

Gopher Planner should be a respectful place to collaborate, regardless of experience, background, identity, disability, or academic standing. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces used for project work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and make room for different experiences.
- Critique code, data, and decisions with specific evidence. Do not attack people.
- Welcome questions, explain unfamiliar context, and help new contributors participate.
- Give constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs, and others' time.
- Keep academic-data disagreements grounded in sources and acknowledge uncertainty.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, intimidation, or targeted insults.
- Sexualized conduct or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private messages, personal details, student records, or credentials without permission.
- Repeated disruption, stalking, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop.

## Reporting concerns

Use an existing private project channel to contact a maintainer or the [repository owner](https://github.com/Troppy2). Include relevant context, links, and the outcome or support you need. Share only information necessary to understand the concern.

If you do not have a private contact route, ask a maintainer to provide one without posting incident details publicly. For example: "I need a private channel for a conduct report." Do not include student data or sensitive allegations in that request.

Security and data-exposure reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Handling concerns

Maintainers should listen, consider context, and limit disclosure to what is needed to address the report. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, but unnecessary public discussion of private reports should be avoided.

Depending on severity and repetition, action may include clarification, a request to edit/remove content, a warning, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Serious threats, privacy violations, or repeated harassment may justify immediate restrictions.

A maintainer personally involved in a report should step back when another uninvolved maintainer is available. A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration privately and provide relevant new context. Retaliation is not acceptable.

Maintainers are responsible for applying these expectations consistently and explaining corrective actions when appropriate. This policy does not promise continuous moderation or a guaranteed response time.
